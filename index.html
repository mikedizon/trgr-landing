Where the agents run and where your data stays

The agents run against your own cloud, not a copy of it. You keep control of the boundary, and the compliance evidence never leaves it.

A scoped, read-only role

You run a template that creates a read-only role in your account. Only the runner in your own account can assume it. TRGR itself has no access path into your account.

Your data stays in your boundary

The data stays inside your environment. The agents read your Config, Security Hub, and live system state, generate the OSCAL package, and flag drift. You receive the artifacts. Your ISSO signs off before anything is reported.

Deterministic verdicts

Every pass or fail comes from a deterministic engine evaluating your collected configuration against the published FedRAMP rules. AI drafts the narrative. Your ISSO reviews and signs before an agency can pull anything.
