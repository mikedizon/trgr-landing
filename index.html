One engine, two ways to buy

The agent suite is one engine. You can buy it built into a TRGR authorization from the start, or bring it to a system that is already authorized. Both keep a human reviewer in the loop.

Build to ATO

The agent suite bundled into a TRGR FedRAMP or NIST 800-171 engagement. We build the architecture, the evidence pipelines, and the OSCAL package. Once the system is live, the agents keep running as continuous authorization.

Continuous authorization

The agents on their own, for a team already running an authorized system or with its own compliance staff. It starts with a lighter onboarding to stand up the read-only role, baseline the boundary, and validate the first package. Your ISSO owns final sign-off. No agent output ships without that signature.

Where the agents run and where your data stays

The agents run against your own cloud, not a copy of it. You keep control of the boundary, and the compliance evidence never leaves it.

A scoped, read-only role

You run a template that creates a read-only role in your account. Only the runner in your own account can assume it. TRGR itself has no access path into your account.

Your data stays in your boundary

The data stays inside your environment. The agents read your Config, Security Hub, and live system state, generate the OSCAL package, and flag drift. You receive the artifacts. Your ISSO signs off before anything is reported.

Deterministic verdicts

Every pass or fail comes from a deterministic engine evaluating your collected configuration against the published rules. AI drafts the narrative. Your ISSO reviews and signs before anything ships.
